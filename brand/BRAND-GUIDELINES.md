# AgentC brand guidelines (v1.0, October 2026)

**Positioning:** The agentic marketing agency where AI agents play every part and a human strategist conducts.
**Tagline:** Agents play. Humans conduct.
**Domain / contact:** agentc.marketing · ask@agentc.marketing
**Technology partner:** SOMIN (somin.ai). Show as "Technology partner SOMIN" with the two-tone SOMIN wordmark (SO teal #2DBF9C, MIN dark/white), never merged into our logo.

## Logo
The mark is a faceted violet orb, an agent, sitting inside an open letter C drawn like a conductor's sweeping gesture. The C holds the orb without closing around it.
Files: `brand/logo.svg` (light backgrounds), `brand/logo-dark.svg` (dark backgrounds), `brand/mark.svg` (icon), `favicon.svg`.
Clear space = height of the mark. Minimum 24 px tall. No recolouring, stretching, outlines or effects.

## Typography
- Headlines: Inter Tight 700, tracking -0.03em, sentence case
- Body: Inter 400/500, 17 px / 1.65
- Labels: Inter 600, 12–13 px, uppercase, 0.2em tracking, accent colour
- Corner radius: 18px

## Personality
- Composed
- Exacting
- Humane
- Candid

## Voice
Do:
- Name who decides, not just what the agent did
- Explain the trade-off behind a recommendation
- Use musical and orchestration metaphors sparingly and precisely

Don't:
- Promise autonomy without accountability
- Talk about replacing people
- Hide behind 'the AI decided'

## Imagery
Glowing faceted violet orbs on deep ink-purple, arranged like sections of an orchestra, with a single human presence giving direction. Calm, luminous, never robotic. All imagery generated with SOMIN image generation; no text in images, no stock, no identifiable real people.

## Calls to action
Every primary CTA ("Brief the agents") opens an email to ask@agentc.marketing.
