What is AgentC?

AgentC is an agentic-AI marketing agency. AI agents run continuous research, creative and optimisation loops, while human strategists own judgement, taste and accountability. The agents run on SOMIN, an AI audience-research platform and AgentC's technology partner, so every recommendation traces back to real audience conversations. The C stands for the conductor: the human who decides what the orchestra plays.

The problem

Automation got faster. Marketing got colder.

Most teams adopted AI the way you would bolt a turbocharger onto a car with no steering. Content volume went up, audience data stayed messy, analytics stayed fragmented and much of the output still needed heavy human revision before it could go anywhere near a customer.

The deeper cost is harder to see on a dashboard. When machines write, target and optimise without anyone holding the brand's conscience, the work stops sounding like people talking to people. We built AgentC to keep the speed of agents and put a human back on the podium.

What we do

Services built around one edge

Agentic audience research

Research agents read real audience conversations continuously and surface tensions, language and moments worth acting on. A strategist decides which ones deserve a brief.

Creative loops with a human edit

Creative agents draft routes, variants and hooks at volume from the research. Our strategists cut, shape and sign off, because taste is not a prompt.

Always-on optimisation

Optimisation agents watch performance, flag fatigue and propose reallocations against profit, not vanity metrics. Changes above an agreed threshold wait for a human yes.

Agent governance design

We map which decisions agents may take alone, which need review and which stay human forever. You get a written operating charter, not a black box.

Marketing team redesign

We help in-house teams reorganise around agents: new roles, rituals and review points. The aim is fewer handoffs and more judgement, not fewer people by default.

Human-connection audits

We review automated journeys for moments where a customer needs a person, not a sequence. Then we design those moments back in.

How it works

Three steps

Brief the conductor

You brief a named AgentC strategist on the business problem, the brand's boundaries and what success means in profit, not impressions.

Humans make the calls

Your strategist reviews, edits and approves at agreed checkpoints, and explains every material decision to you in plain language.

About

Why we called it AgentC

AgentC began with a simple observation: the agencies adopting AI fastest were also producing the most forgettable work. The agents were capable. Nobody was conducting them.

So we split the job cleanly. Agents do what agents are good at: reading thousands of conversations, drafting hundreds of variants, watching performance every hour. Humans do what humans are good at: deciding what a brand should stand for, noticing when something is technically right and emotionally wrong, and taking responsibility.

The C is for the conductor. It is also a reminder of the trade-off we refuse to make: when efficiency and humanity pull in different directions, we choose humanity and design the efficiency around it.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What does AgentC actually do?

AgentC is a marketing agency where AI agents run the repetitive loops of research, creative production and campaign optimisation, and human strategists make the decisions. You get continuous output and monitoring from the agents, and a named strategist who owns judgement, brand fit and accountability for every material call.

What does the C stand for?

The conductor. An orchestra can play brilliantly without the conductor touching an instrument, but someone has to decide the tempo, the interpretation and when to stop. At AgentC that person is a human strategist, and the agents are the orchestra.

Which technology do your agents run on?

Our agents run on SOMIN, an AI audience-research platform that is our technology partner. SOMIN's agents analyse real audience conversations and link every insight back to the posts that support it, which lets our strategists verify the evidence before acting on it.

Will agents publish anything without human approval?

Only what your operating charter allows. We agree in writing which actions agents may take alone, such as pausing a fatigued ad within budget, and which always wait for a human, such as new messaging, new audiences or anything sensitive. Brand-new creative always gets a human edit.

Is AgentC trying to replace marketing teams?

No. We believe agents should take the drudgery so people can do more of the work that needs judgement, empathy and taste. When we help redesign a team, the goal is better decisions and fewer handoffs, not headcount cuts dressed up as innovation.
